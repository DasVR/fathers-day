A Letter for You, Dad

Dear Dad,

I know your work takes up much of your time lately, and honestly it's rewarding to me personally to see you as this hard of a worker. Even with the ups and downs of consistently taking calls and writing emails, reporting to meetings and helping out with trainings, you do so well.

I decided to make this for you, and us as a whole. Not because anything happened, or because I wanted something from you, but because I wanted somewhere for you to be able to go to reflect; not just on work or other things that can overwhelm you. It's just for us, full of some of my favorite memories and photos of you and I, things I don't say out loud enough. A place for you to explore, just to see how far you have come just being my father.

You have been the most important person in my life, and you haven't ever given up on trying for me and for us. When I was little and would freak out over stupid fears, you would always laugh at them, but you still would console me and help me with them. When I got stressed and talked a bunch about my friends or about school, you would listen. Whenever I was upset with something or at you, you still wouldn't hesitate to try and help, even with the consequences that came with it, whether they were on you or me. Most of the things I am good at were taught by you and encouraged by you. The things that I learned through the early years of being a kid to now were mainly from you. I mean, I wouldn't have been able to read this well if it weren't for all of the stories you would make me read and read to me. You were always by my side, you always are there; showing up to every little event that took place that was involved with me, always being my #1 supporter.

I'm fifteen now, and every year I understand a little bit more about what it means to be your son, what you sacrifice, and who you are as my father. I am so extremely proud, grateful, and overjoyed to be your son even when I don't show it, because at the end of the day we all know I'm not mature enough to be as grateful as I should be, but I still am as much as I can understand. I hope that you are able to scroll through this whenever you need to get away from something or just need a breather, whenever you need one.

I will make sure to update this as we get more photos, so it can forever stay updated and not feel like a relic.

Love you forever,
Arriq
